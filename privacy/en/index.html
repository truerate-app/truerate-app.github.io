Privacy Policy

App: TrueRate (Android) · Last updated: November 13, 2026

In short: TrueRate does not collect, transmit or share personal data. Everything you enter stays on your phone. There are no accounts, no developer servers, no ads and no analytics.

Who we are

TrueRate is developed by an independent developer. For any question, write to truerate.app@gmail.com.

What data the app uses

The app stores only what you enter: jobs, hours, amounts, material expenses, other costs and settings. This data is saved in a local database on your device and is not accessible to the developer.

What we do not do

  • We do not require you to create an account.
  • We do not send your data to servers or cloud services. The app does not request Internet access permission.
  • We do not use analytics, tracking, advertising or automatic crash reporting.
  • We do not enable Android's automatic cloud backup for the app's data.
  • We do not use third-party libraries or services that collect data.

Permissions requested

  • Notifications: to show the end-of-day reminder. The notification is generated on your device.
  • Device startup: to reschedule the reminder after a restart.

The app does not request any other permissions (location, contacts, camera, microphone and the like).

Export, import and job summary

You can export your data to a backup file (JSON) and generate an end-of-job summary. These actions happen only when you start them. You choose where to save the file or which app to share it with. Once it leaves the app, the file is subject to the rules of the service or app you choose, over which TrueRate has no control.

Retention and deletion

Data stays on your device until you delete it in the app or uninstall TrueRate, in which case it is erased. Exported files remain wherever you saved them and must be deleted manually.

Website and link to this page

This page is hosted on GitHub Pages. When you open it, GitHub may log technical access data (such as your IP address) according to its own privacy statement. The link in the app's settings opens this page in your device's browser; the app itself makes no connections.

Children

TrueRate is not directed at children and does not collect personal data from anyone, children included.

Your rights

Because the developer neither collects nor has access to your data, it always remains under your control: you can view, edit, export or delete it directly in the app. For any privacy-related request, write to the address above.

Changes to this policy

If the app changes how it handles data in the future (for example, with new features), we will update this page and the date at the top before releasing the change.
